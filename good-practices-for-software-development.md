# Good Practices for Software Development

**Question-1 Which point(s) were new to you?**  
Ans- Batching questions into one message was new to me. I usually send my doubts one by one. Now I understand that putting all my questions in one clear message saves time and makes it easier for others to understand and reply properly.

**Question-2 Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?**  
Ans-  
Inform the team early if I see any delay or blocker.  
Give small and regular updates about my work.  
Use the group chat more instead of sending private messages.  
Ask for help quickly if I get stuck instead of trying to solve everything alone.  
Be clear and direct while explaining my progress or issues.
