## Prevention of Sexual Harassment

### Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

**1) Physical Harassment**
- Serious physical actions  
- Unwanted or inappropriate touching  
- Forced closeness or intimacy  
- Blocking someone’s movement  

**2) Verbal Harassment**
- Sexually suggestive or offensive comments  
- Personal or intrusive questions  
- Unwanted requests or demands  
- Teasing, whistling, or mocking sounds  
- Insulting or degrading words  

**3) Visual Harassment**
- Harassment through digital platforms  
- Obscene or sexual gestures  
- Sharing explicit images or videos  
- Staring in a threatening or uncomfortable way  

---

### Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

**1) If I face harassment myself**
- I will speak up and say no clearly.  
- I will note down all details and save proof.  
- I will report it to the Internal Committee (IC).  

**2) If I witness harassment happening to others**
- I will intervene or call for help if it is safe.  
- I will support the person affected.  
- I will report what I saw to authorities.  

**3) If harassment happens repeatedly**
- I will inform the Internal Committee again.  
- I will ask for higher-level action if needed.  
- I will use SHe-Box if no action is taken.
